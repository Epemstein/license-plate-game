How to Play
Objective
  • Solve 10 plates as fast as you can
  • To solve a plate, enter a word that contains the license plate letters in order (but not necessarily consecutively)
  • You can press Skip, but it adds a time penalty: +5s, then +10s, +15s, etc.
Examples
RSE
rise
R, then S, then E
coarse
R, then S, then E (letters don't need to be consecutive)
brisket
R appears first, then S, then E (can have letters in between)
surmise
First S comes BEFORE the R - wrong order!
